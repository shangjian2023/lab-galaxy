## Getty Open Content artwork

The editorial atmosphere images under `public/images/atmospheres/` and `public/images/rooms/` were recovered from this project's own archived frontend release. They are locally stored, resized WebP derivatives of images offered through the Getty Open Content Program; CSS overlays and responsive crops are applied for decorative backgrounds.

The original source URLs, processing notes, dimensions, checksums, and reuse records are preserved alongside the assets in:

- `public/images/atmospheres/ATTRIBUTION.json`
- `public/images/rooms/ATTRIBUTION.json`

The team background is J. M. W. Turner's *Van Tromp, going about to please his Masters, Ships a Sea, getting a Good Wetting* (1844), The J. Paul Getty Museum, accession 93.PA.32. Source and reuse information are included in the room attribution file. These artwork backgrounds are independent of the HALOFLUX visualization license above.

## AI-generated tactile card assets

The eight impasto backgrounds in `public/images/card-textures/` and eleven decorative objects in `public/images/card-artifacts/` were generated for this project on 2026-11-10 with Magpie `generate_image`, using `sui-xiang/gpt-image-2.6-flare`. They depict generic interface decoration, not real documents, laboratory equipment, inventory, or experimental results.

Card titles, statistics, evidence and controls are rendered as HTML rather than baked into the images. The generated source images were resized and converted to WebP; decorative objects are checked for an actual alpha channel and transparent pixels. Generation parameters, processing details and alpha-check results are recorded in each directory's `ATTRIBUTION.json`. Temporary provider delivery URLs are intentionally not retained.

## AI-generated Atlas editorial engravings

The seven original engravings in `public/images/atlas-engravings/` were generated for this project on 2026-11-10 with the same Magpie image2.6 provider. They provide independent visual metaphors for the frontispiece, document collection, knowledge distillation, graph connection, evidence-based questions, collaboration and the final library reveal. No Hermes logo, character, proprietary artwork, application screenshot, text or brand composition is used.

These illustrations are decorative and do not represent real report pages, experimental outcomes, software screenshots or evidence. Their local WebP dimensions, generation method and usage boundary are recorded in `public/images/atlas-engravings/ATTRIBUTION.json`.
